New Volcengine accounts get 20 hours of free recognition. Need more? Buy more hours and select the package 录音文件识别 (recording-file recognition) — not 流式语音识别 (streaming speech recognition). Please don't pick the wrong package.

Privacy: your speech is uploaded to Volcengine (Doubao) for recognition — it is not processed entirely on this device. Please keep this in mind for sensitive audio.
